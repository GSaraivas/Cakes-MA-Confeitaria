# Project Brief & PRD: Cakes'MA Confeitaria Digital Presence

## 1. Project Overview
**Client:** Cakes'MA Confeitaria
**Industry:** Premium Artisanal Bakery / Luxury Confectionery
**Objective:** Create a high-end, high-conversion digital experience that reflects the brand's core values of artisanal quality, sophistication, and emotional connection.

## 2. Brand Identity & Visual Language
### 2.1 Core Values
- **Artisanal Excellence:** Every product is a handcrafted piece of art.
- **Sophistication:** A minimalist, premium aesthetic inspired by brands like Apple and Aesop.
- **Affection:** Warm, emotional storytelling that connects with clients' special moments.

### 2.2 Visual Assets
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (Headlines), Inter (Body).
- **Color Palette:** 
  - Primary: Creme (#F8F3EE), White (#FFFFFF).
  - Secondary: Chocolate (#4A2E25), Caramel (#B78652).
  - Neutrals: Warm Gray (#8B8178).
- **Photography Style:** High-end food photography, shallow depth of field, warm natural lighting, focus on texture and detail.

## 3. Product Requirements (PRD)
### 3.1 Target Audience
- High-income individuals looking for exclusive cakes for weddings, birthdays, and corporate events.
- Gift-givers seeking premium artisanal baskets and gourmet treats.

### 3.2 Key Features
- **Responsive Landing Page:** Mobile-first, desktop-optimized experience.
- **Immersive Hero Section:** Immediate emotional impact with high-quality visual storytelling.
- **Curated Product Showcase:** Categories for custom cakes, brigadeiros, desserts, and gift baskets.
- **Social Proof Integration:** Testimonials and performance metrics (+500 happy clients).
- **Direct Conversion Path:** Integrated WhatsApp lead generation.

### 3.3 User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Minimal Friction:** Clear hierarchy and prominent CTAs above the fold.
- **Visual Feedback:** Micro-interactions on hover and scroll-triggered animations.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA compliance, legible contrast, and keyboard navigation.

## 4. Technical Specifications
- **Stack:** Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (No frameworks).
- **Performance Goals:** Lighthouse score 90+, SEO 95+.
- **Interactions:** Smooth scroll, fade-in reveals, dynamic glassmorphism header.

## 5. Roadmap
- **Phase 1:** Landing Page Design & Content Strategy (Complete).
- **Phase 2:** Product Gallery Expansion & Detailed Product Pages.
- **Phase 3:** Online Order Management & Customer Portal.
